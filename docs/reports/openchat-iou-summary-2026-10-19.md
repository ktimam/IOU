# OpenChat and IOU: chronological work summary

**Coverage:** the conversation and retained project evidence from August 1 through October 19, 2026. Conversation-only reports are identified as such; a period does not imply that every action in that row happened on one exact day. Earlier successes qualify their recorded source, device and workflow, not every later build.

Read the [detailed retrospective](openchat-iou-retrospective-2026-10-19.md) for causes, decisions and evidence, and the [chat export notes](../../chats/2026-10-19-openchat-iou/README.md) for the export's provenance and limits. This is a retrospective, not authorization for additional features, deployment, recovery charges or dependency changes. Private account, chat, sheet, device and credential identifiers are intentionally omitted.

- [Frontend hosted run](https://github.com/ktimam/open-chat/actions/runs/39164532047): passed. [Scoped hosted run](https://github.com/ktimam/open-chat/actions/runs/39164532063): six functional jobs passed; dependency/advisory job failed and was explicitly disclosed/deferred for publication.

**Recovery-cost boundary:** local real-crypto tests observed no additional paid-capable RPCs for ordinary manager recovery, reload, reconnect and inbox use after initial bootstrap. They do not establish a measured zero-cost mainnet operation. The [recovery validation report](../account-vault-browser-validation.md) expressly retains the incomplete real-user capture, synthetic-auth/autofill limits and failed physical Microsoft PRF attempts.

**Retrospective lesson:** distinguish extraction from schema validation, schema validation from rendered-card correctness, card correctness from encrypted delivery, delivery from ledger Save, source tests from the served/installed artifact, and historical device evidence from the final release. The [detailed retrospective](openchat-iou-retrospective-2026-10-19.md) expands these boundaries rather than converting old failures, skips or incomplete checks into passes.
